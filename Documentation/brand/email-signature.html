Adding it to your mail app

Gmail (web)

  1. Press Copy signature under the one you want.
  2. In Gmail: the gear icon (top right), See all settings, the General tab, scroll to Signature.
  3. Create new, give it a name (for example "IMKAN"), click in the box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and press Save Changes.

Outlook (new Outlook for Windows, and Outlook on the web)

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook: the gear icon (top right), Account, Signatures, + New signature.
  3. Name it, click in the box, paste (Ctrl+V), and press Save.
  4. Under Select default signatures, pick it for new messages and for replies, and press Save.

Outlook (classic, for Windows)

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook: File, Options, Mail, Signatures..., New, name it.
  3. Click in the edit box, paste (Ctrl+V), pick it as the default for new messages and replies, and press OK.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Press Copy signature.
  2. In Mail: Mail, Settings, Signatures, pick the account, press +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, then paste (Cmd+V) into the right-hand box.

Dark mode

When a reader's mail app is in dark mode, it recolours the text to keep it readable. The logo keeps its solid tile (white, or plum-black for the dark signature), so it always shows as designed.

If the logo does not show

Some mail apps hide images from new senders until the reader allows them; that is the reader's setting, not the signature. The logo loads from www.imkan.ai, so it needs an internet connection.
